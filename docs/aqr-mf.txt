AQR Managed Futures UCITS: share classes, fees and what the prospectus settles
==============================================================================

Read this before touching any LU1103.../LU1662.../LU2622... entry in
assets.json, or the aqrmf*/aqrIAET*/aqrIAE1FT* recipes in pkg/simgen.

Primary source: AQR UCITS Funds prospectus, Supplement 2 (AQR Managed Futures
UCITS Fund), "Summary of Shares, Fees and Expenses" tables and the general
"Fees and Expenses" / "Performance Fee" sections. Cross-checked against the
semi-annual report to 30 September 2025 (share class NAVs, note 6 Fees and
Expenses, and the Swiss annex Total Net Expense Ratio table) and against the
Financial Times tearsheet of each class. Read 2026-08-08.


1. The one fact that explains everything
----------------------------------------

Every EUR class of this sub-fund holds the SAME portfolio. What separates
their published NAVs is their fee schedule and nothing else. So:

  - comparing two classes on NAV performance measures their fee difference,
    never the manager;
  - converting one class's NAV into another's is a fee transformation, which
    is what both new backcasts do;
  - a class whose fees are NOT levied inside its NAV looks like a better fund.

That last case is real here and it is RAEF.

3. List price against what actually reaches the NAV
---------------------------------------------------

  class    prospectus list        FT ongoing   audited Swiss TNER (30/09/2025)
  RAEF     1.29% (1.00+0.24+0.05)   0.23%      0.24% before perf fee, no perf fee
  B        0.79% (0.60+0.18+0.01)   0.73%      0.74% + 1.96% perf fee = 2.70%
  IAET     0.84% (0.60+0.23+0.01)   0.78%      0.78% + 0.71% perf fee = 1.49%
  IAE1FT   1.26% (1.00+0.25+0.01)   1.26%      not yet reported (launched 2026-05)
  A        0.79%                    0.79%      0.73% + 0.48% perf fee = 1.21%

IAE1FT matches its list to the basis point. RAEF does not, by a full point.

Why: the prospectus provides that the Investment Management Fee is "calculated
at an annual rate equal to a percentage of the average daily Net Asset Value
of the Fund ... accrued daily and ... payable BY THE FUND monthly in arrears",
and separately that "the Investment Manager may from time to time, and at its
sole discretion, and out of its own resources decide to WAIVE, REDUCE OR
RETURN all or a portion of the Investment Management Fees and/or Performance
Fee to some or all Shareholders ... their agents or to intermediaries".

There is therefore NO mechanism by which the management fee is billed outside
the fund: it is always inside the NAV. A class whose list is 1.29% and whose
audited TNER is 0.24% (which is its expense cap alone) is a class whose
management fee is being WAIVED. That is consistent with RAEF being, by the
prospectus's own definition, substantially a class for the manager's own
senior management.

Consequences:
  - a RAEF holder pays what the NAV shows, about 0.23%/yr, NOT 1.23%;
  - the waiver is at AQR's sole discretion and revocable without shareholder
    approval, so RAEF's cost can go to 1.29%/yr at any time;
  - the prospectus also allows per-holder rebates negotiated privately and
    states the Fund "cannot, and is under no duty to, enforce equality of
    treatment between Shareholders", so another holder's economics need not
    be ours.

An earlier version of this catalog pinned RAEF at 1.31% (its list) and
described it as beating every cheaper sibling. Both were wrong: the pin is
0.23% and the "outperformance" is the waiver.


4. Performance fee mechanics (identical for every class that pays one)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Rate 10%, hurdle EUR STR for the EUR classes, ML 3 Month T-Bill for USD,
    SONIA for GBP.
  - High-on-High model, computed DAILY: the day's change in gross NAV per
    share minus the day's change in an adjusted hurdle, times the rate, times
    shares outstanding. A NEGATIVE hurdle return is floored at zero.
  - Calculation Period = the twelve months ending 31 March. Accrued daily as
    an expense of the class, crystallised at period end only if cumulative
    overperformance is positive.
  - LOSS CARRYFORWARD over "the whole life of the relevant Class of Shares",
    which "cannot be reset". Losses must be recovered before any fee is
    payable again. This is a genuine shareholder protection and rules out the
    reset games some funds play.
  - Subscriptions do not create an artificial fee ("artificial increases
    resulting from new subscriptions and/or exchanges will not be taken into
    account").

Measured cost, from the accounts: 0.64 pts of average class NAV for IAET in
the half-year to 30/09/2025 alone; 1.19 for class B; 2.03 (class B) and 2.07
(class RAE) for the full year to 31 March 2026; 1.58 for class A over the same
year.


5. Dealing terms
----------------

Daily dealing, every Business Day. Dealing request deadline 13:00 Luxembourg
time on the Dealing Day. Subscription settlement within 3 Business Days;
redemption normally within 10 Business Days, targeted at 3. No subscription or
redemption fee in the supplement's tables for these classes. Swing pricing may
be applied.

Leverage: the notional figure is huge (the supplement explains the sum-of-
notionals methodology and that it is dominated by 3-month interest rate
futures, "not necessarily representative of economic risk").


6. What this validated in pofo, and what it corrected
-----------------------------------------------------

Validated:
  - IAET and class B EUR pay the SAME performance fee (10%, EUR STR,
    High-on-High, 31 March), which is the whole justification for making B the
    donor of the IAET backcast: a performance fee cannot be removed from a NAV
    after the fact, and does not need to be when the target pays the same one.
  - The 0.05%/yr alignment between them is the prospectus's own arithmetic
    (IAET cap 0.23 against B's 0.18, everything else equal). Measured
    independently at 0.096 pts/yr over their 672 common days, correlation
    0.9991.
  - IAE1FT is flat with no performance fee, so RAEF (also flat) is the right
    donor; the 1.03%/yr conversion is 1.26 minus 0.23. Measured at 0.995
    pts/yr over their 59 common days, correlation 1.0000.
  - The catalog pins 0.78 (IAET) and 1.26 (IAE1FT) are correct.

Corrected:
  - RAEF's pin, 1.31 to 0.23, and its notes.
  - The claim that RAEF beats its siblings on merit.
  - The open question left in an earlier pass ("waived or billed outside?") is
    settled: waived, never billed outside.

Left standing, and worth re-checking at each refresh:
  - aqrRAEFOngoing (0.0023) depends on a discretionary waiver. If FT's ongoing
    charge for LU1662501532 ever prints near 1.29%, the waiver is gone: RAEF's
    pin, the IAE1FT conversion and the IAE1FT backcast all move together.
  - IAE1FT has 59 days of real quotes. Its validation window will stay thin
    for a year; nothing else about it is uncertain.
