# Chimera (three thirds) -- one capital-efficient engine, gold, trend, in equal parts
#
# Idea: the All-Weather promise (a book that does not depend on the regime)
#   kept with the fewest moving parts. Three lines of equal weight, each a
#   different SOURCE of return: a 90/60 efficient core for the growth regime
#   (stocks plus the duration that pays in a deflationary crash), physical
#   gold for the regime nothing else covers (inflation, currency stress),
#   and managed futures for the crisis that unfolds over months. The core is
#   levered so the two diversifiers can be held at full size without the
#   equity budget paying for them; the diversifiers are NOT levered, on
#   purpose (see "why not a stacked gold line").
#
# Lineage: the third child of Typhon and Echidna, after Hydra
#   (hydra-five-engines-ucits.txt, one head per engine) and Cerberus
#   (cerberus-three-heads.txt, three even thirds of NOTIONAL). Chimera is
#   three even thirds of CAPITAL: lion, goat and serpent on one body, and
#   the one that fits a retail account without any opt-up (every line UCITS
#   or with a UCITS twin named in its comment).
#
# What 100 EUR holds (notional 120): 36 US equity, 24 US Treasuries
#   (intermediate ladder, futures), 30 gold, 30 managed futures. Regime
#   coverage by weight: growth 36, deflation 24, inflation 60, crisis 60.
#
# Measured (USD, 1996-03 to 2026-09, sim:on, 180-day rebalancing) against
#   the S&P 500 over the same window:
#     CAGR 10.7 % vs 10.4 %; volatility 9.8 % vs 19.0 %; Sharpe 1.08 vs 0.61;
#     max drawdown -19.4 % vs -55.3 %; longest recovery 1.8 y vs 6.1 y;
#     worst rolling 5y +3.4 %/yr vs -8.2 %; beta 0.30.
#   Stress windows (total return): dotcom 2000-03..2003-03 -5.5 % (S&P -44 %),
#     2007-10..2009-03 -21.4 %, COVID 2020-02..03 -12.5 %, the 2022 rate
#     shock -10.2 %. The gold bear of 2013-2015 is its worst regime: +4.8 %/yr
#     with a -9.5 % drawdown, i.e. the price of holding the inflation hedge
#     through two years where it was not needed.
#
# Why not a stacked gold line (GDE, 90 % equity + 90 % gold on one dollar).
#   Measured, same window: 34 NTSX / 33 GDE / 33 KMLM returns 12.8 %/yr,
#   but at 13.1 % volatility, a -30.5 % drawdown, beta 0.55, and a WORSE
#   result on every stress window above (dotcom -12.8 %, 2008 -31.7 %,
#   2022 -13.6 %). The stacked gold line spends its capital efficiency on
#   more equity (60 of the 100), not on more diversification, and the
#   book's deflation cover falls to 20. Same Sharpe (0.98) as the old
#   four-line version of this file, three points more return bought with
#   three points more volatility: a fine growth book, not an all-weather.
#   The frontier sibling (hydra-five-engines-capital-efficient.txt) is
#   where that trade is made on purpose.
#
# Why no broad commodity line. The earlier version of this file held 15 %
#   of a commodity index next to the trend fund. Managed futures already
#   trade the same contracts, long AND short, and the trend fund carried
#   the 2022 inflation year on its own (+24 %); a long-only commodity
#   basket adds the roll cost and the 2014-2020 bear for a cover the
#   sleeve already provides. Dropping it took the book from four lines to
#   three and improved every risk figure.
#
# Where it fails. A prolonged stagflation with gold AND trend both out of
#   phase (1970s-style, the trend fund's backcast reaches 1996 only, so
#   that decade is not in the test); a slow lost decade in the equity
#   core, which the two diversifiers soften without profiting from; and
#   model risk on the trend line, whose pre-2020 history is a donor chain
#   of other managers' NAVs (see pkg/datasets/simdata/KMLM.csv's header
#   and docs/trend-reconstruction-design.md for the error budget).
#
# Buy route (EU retail): NTSX -> IE000KF370H3 (the UCITS twin); GOLD ->
#   IGLN (IE00B4ND3602, physical ETC); KMLM -> iMGP DBi Managed Futures
#   (LU2951555403, EUR class) or the EUR-hedged AQR class (LU2622190622);
#   the KMLM line is the US-listed index replicator the backcast is
#   validated against.
#meta rebalance:180
#meta sim:on

30    KMLM            # managed futures (UCITS: iMGP DBi LU2951555403): the crisis that unfolds over months
