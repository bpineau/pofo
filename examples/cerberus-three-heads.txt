# Cerberus (three heads) -- the S&P 500's return without the parts that break the holder
#
# STATUS: nearly buyable. Five of the six lines are UCITS funds a French
#   retail account can hold today; the sixth (GDE, 24%) is US-listed with
#   no PRIIPs KID, and its accessibility is this file's one standing
#   problem (the access ledger below is the state of the art). Cerberus is
#   the hydra's sibling, in the family and in the myth: Hydra and Cerberus
#   are both children of Typhon and Echidna, and this build is the same
#   engine philosophy regrouped into THREE EVEN HEADS of notional exposure.
#   Its job is the watchdog's: guard the floor while the owner sleeps.
#
# Thesis in one line: the S&P 500's 30-year return at half its volatility,
#   a third of its drawdown and a sixth of its time under water, packaged
#   as three balanced notional thirds (equity / trend / bonds+gold) with a
#   minority of direct US exposure.
#
# ## What 100 EUR actually holds (the exposure ledger)
#
#   17 NTSG   -> 15.3 world equity + 10.2 sovereign futures (~7y duration)
#    6 AVWS   ->  6   global small-cap value equity
#   28 MFEH   -> 28   managed-futures trend (replication, EUR-hedged)
#   16 AQR MF -> 16   managed-futures trend (direct TSMOM, EUR-hedged)
#   24 GDE    -> 21.6 US large-cap equity + 21.6 gold futures
#    9 MTH    ->  9   euro sovereigns at ~19y duration
#   ------------------------------------------------------------------
#   Total     ~43 equity + ~44 trend + ~41 bonds-and-gold  = ~128%
#
#   That last line IS the design: three even heads, one third of notional
#   each (34/34/32), the hard-ballast head split half nominal duration
#   (19.2 bonds) and half gold (21.6). The ~28 stacked points are financed
#   inside NTSG and GDE at roughly the T-bill rate; the frontier sibling
#   (hydra-five-engines-capital-efficient.txt) keeps the full case for
#   embedded leverage over margin, boxes and daily-reset ETFs.
#
# ## Where it stands in the hydra family
#   Same five engines as the hydras; what changed is the passport and the
#   currency doctrine. Three swaps against the frontier build:
#   - ZPRV -> AVWS: the small-value sleeve goes global (31% non-US) instead
#     of US-only. The frontier file measured the swap a wash on returns;
#     here the tie-breaker is the whole point of the build (minority US).
#   - RAEF -> the AQR flat-fee I class (IAE1FT): same fund, but a share
#     class whose published charge is real and durable. RAEF's 0.23%
#     ongoing figure is a discretionary management-fee waiver that can end
#     any day; IAE1FT's 1.26% is what the NAV actually pays, and the class
#     is reachable through ordinary brokers (IBKR carries it).
#   - ZROZ -> MTH: the deflation-duration head moves from a 27y USD
#     zero-coupon to a 19y euro sovereign book. Deliberate doctrine, not a
#     compromise: the USD flight-to-quality premise behind a Treasury
#     sleeve was re-examined on the very windows it is supposed to pay in
#     and did not survive the check, so the defensive duration stays in
#     the liability currency instead of paying dollar volatility on top of
#     the rate bet.
#   Net: direct US exposure is a minority stake, 40% by geography and 41%
#   by currency (EUR 54%), against a family habit of USD-heavy books.
#
# ## Regime map: what hurts, and which head answers
#
#   Growth boom            ~43% equity across three engines (NTSG world,
#                          AVWS global small value, GDE US large).
#   Disinflation/recession NTSG's overlay (~10 points intermediate
#                          duration) + MTH + trend rotating long.
#   Deflation crash (2008) MTH long-end convexity in euros + trend short
#                          risk assets.
#   Inflation shock        Trend long commodities/short bonds + 21.6
#                          points of gold via GDE.
#   Choppy stagflation     Gold (trend whipsaws without a trend).
#   Prolonged equity bear  Trend crisis alpha, 44 points of it.
#
#   Measured coverage (share of weight): growth 43%, inflation 72%,
#   crisis 75%, deflation 19%. Deflation is the thin quadrant, as in the
#   frontier build (21%): the repair, if wanted, is MTH toward 15-20 (the
#   sweep below prices it at ~0.28 pts of CAGR per 5 points).
#
# ## The five engines (weights, and sane ranges from the per-line sweep)
#
# ENGINE 1 -- NTSG, equity + duration (17%; range 10-25).
#   90% developed-world equities + 60% sovereign futures, UCITS
#   (IE00077IIPQ8). The sweep reads nearly flat Sharpe (1.05-1.07) across
#   0-25 and a ceiling past ~25 (Sharpe 1.03 and falling, drawdown -21% by
#   30): the ceiling is empirical. The floor is a priori, as in every
#   hydra: the sweep would happily hold 0% NTSG, but the worst-5y floor
#   thins (+0.65% at 0) and a 30-year plan needs the world-equity presence.
#
# ENGINE 2 -- AVWS, global small-cap value (6%; range 0-10).
#   Size and value premia in the value-weighted form, global edition
#   (IE0003R87OG3), carrying the non-US tail ZPRV lacks. The most
#   expensive risk in the book, empirically: each 5 points buys ~0.13 pts
#   of CAGR and costs ~1.4 pts of drawdown and ~0.4 pts of worst-5y; by
#   20% the worst-5y is negative. The ceiling is empirical and tight.
#
# ENGINES 3a/3b -- trend, TWO models (28% + 16%; 44 total).
#   The largest head, and knowingly above the frontier file's 40-point
#   comfort edge and its ~22-per-leg cap. The overweight leg is carried on
#   the manager's record, not the share class's: MFEH (LU3359622902,
#   0.75%/yr) is DBi's SG CTA replication, the same engine that has run
#   DBMF since 2019 with the longest replication record in the business,
#   and the class adds only an EUR hedge on top. Engine 3b is AQR's direct
#   TSMOM (Managed Futures UCITS, flat-fee I class IAE1FT LU2622190622,
#   EUR-hedged). Both legs hedged: an unhedged managed-futures sleeve is
#   trend plus a long-dollar position (the frontier file measures the
#   dollar leg at +3 pts of volatility for no return). The sweep is
#   monotone in Sharpe up to 45% on either leg, which is exactly the
#   in-sample flattery of a shared reconstruction the family's blind-spot
#   notes warn about: the 44-point total and the 28/16 split are a priori.
#
# ENGINE 4 -- GDE, gold stacked on equity (24%; range 15-30).
#   90% S&P 500 + 90% gold futures (US97717Y5684). The CAGR engine: the
#   sweep pays +0.39 pts of CAGR and +0.48 pts of worst-5y per 5 points,
#   at ~0.5 pts of volatility each. The floor is empirical: below ~15 the
#   worst-5y goes negative and the build no longer matches the index's
#   return, i.e. Cerberus stops being Cerberus. Sharpe peaks at 5-15 and
#   volatility makes the ceiling call. NOT UCITS; see the access ledger.
#
# ENGINE 5 -- MTH, euro deflation duration (9%; range 5-20).
#   Amundi Euro Government Bond 25+Y (LU1686832194, 0.07%/yr, ~19y
#   duration): recession/deflation convexity in the liability currency.
#   The sweep prices each 5 points at ~0.28 pts of CAGR for ~0.35 pts less
#   volatility and a slowly rising worst-5y, up to a cliff past ~35 where
#   2022-style duration losses dominate (drawdown -23%, recovery 3.9y at
#   45%): both ends of the range are empirical. The euro book carries
#   OAT/BTP spread risk (31% France, 15% Italy): see blind spot (a).
#
# ## GDE access ledger (the one standing problem, measured 2026-08)
#   No UCITS stacks gold on equity today. What exists, in order:
#   - HOLD GDE ANYWAY: requires an account that clears PRIIPs, i.e. MiFID
#     professional opt-up (2 of 3: 500k EUR portfolio, trading frequency,
#     professional experience) or a non-EU account. The options-exercise
#     route that unlocks big US ETFs does not work here: GDE has no liquid
#     option chain.
#   - SYNTHESIZE IT, UCITS-only: 24 GDE -> 10.8 CL2 (Amundi MSCI USA 2x
#     Daily, FR0010755611, PEA-eligible) + 13.2 IGLN (physical gold,
#     IE00B4ND3602). Measured on the shared 2009-2026 window: CAGR 10.28%
#     vs 10.84%, volatility 8.70% vs 9.58%, Sharpe 1.13 vs 1.09, same
#     -19.5% drawdown, worst-5y +0.18% vs +0.95%. The -0.56 pts of CAGR
#     buy the passport; read them as a floor, not the full price: the
#     window starts mid-2009 and never charges CL2's daily-reset decay a
#     2000-02-style chop, and 8.4 points of gold notional are missing
#     (gold at 1x cannot fund both halves from 24 points of capital).
#   - DE-STACK IT (12 IGLN + NTSG raised to 29): refuted. CAGR 9.03%
#     (-1.6 pts), worst-5y -0.22%: it breaks both properties the build
#     exists for. Documented so nobody retries it.
#   - WATCH LIST: a WisdomTree Efficient Gold UCITS stays the clean
#     unlock. Two live signals: Return Stacked announced a UCITS rollout
#     with HANetf targeted at 2026 (nothing shipped as of 2026-08), and
#     their RSSX (US stocks + gold/bitcoin 100/100, US-listed 2025-05) is
#     a near-GDE with a bitcoin kicker; a UCITS RSSX would do this job.
#
# ## Currency
#   EUR 54% / USD 41% / other 5% of look-through exposure: both trend legs
#   are EUR-hedged share classes and the whole duration head is euro
#   sovereigns, so the dollar remains only where it is paid for (the
#   equity premium inside NTSG and GDE). Hedging is not free forward (the
#   USD-EUR carry gap, ~1.5%/yr at 2026-08 rates); the build accepts that
#   as the price of a defensive book that pays in the liability currency.
#   AVWS quotes in EUR on Xetra but that is cosmetic (its basket is ~55%
#   USD); no hedged class exists, and at 6 points it would move the book's
#   currency mix by ~2 points: not worth an instrument change.
#
# ## Blind spots
#   (a) A euro-sovereign stress (2011): MTH is then exposure, not hedge
#       (31% France, 15% Italy; no Bund-only long ETF exists). The USD
#       zero-coupon dodged this one and caught dollar volatility instead;
#       this build chose its side of that trade.
#   (b) DEFLATION is the thin quadrant (19% of weight): a bust that trend
#       misses leans on 9 points of MTH plus NTSG's overlay.
#   (c) 44 points of capital in trend, above the family's 40-point
#       live-record edge, and the deep history of both legs shares one
#       reconstruction: the backtest cannot price model divergence and
#       flatters the sleeve. Treat it as a regime study, not a promise.
#   (d) Product risk: GDE is small and young; MFEH is younger and smaller
#       still (~EUR 11m, weeks of live NAV, Xetra only, France
#       registration to verify before buying). DBMFE (LU2951555403) is the
#       same book unhedged if MFEH is unreachable; the frontier file
#       prices that fallback at +3 pts of sleeve volatility.
#   (e) Fee stack: 0.53%/yr weighted, the trend legs being 0.75% and
#       1.26%. That is the price of published charges that are real
#       (RAEF's 0.23% is a waiver, not a fee schedule).
#   (f) Embedded financing: the ~28 stacked points pay T-bill inside the
#       funds; when cash is at 5% that is ~1.4%/yr of drag before the
#       overlays earn anything (2022-23).
#
# ## Honesty note on the WEIGHTS
